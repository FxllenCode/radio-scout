# Reference

Codes differ from one agency to the next, so this page is yours to fill in.
What follows is only what is genuinely the same everywhere.

## Our codes

Replace this table with the codes your agencies actually use. Radio-Scout
does not ship a national list, because there is not one: the same ten-code
means different things a county apart.

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 10-4 | Acknowledged |

Many agencies now speak plain English instead. The National Incident
Management System asks for plain language whenever more than one agency is
working an incident, so you may hear fewer codes than you expect.

## Twenty-four-hour time

Dispatch reads times on the 24-hour clock: "fourteen-thirty" is 2:30 in the
afternoon, and "zero-six-hundred" is 6 in the morning.
